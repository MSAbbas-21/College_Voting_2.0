Democracy & Integrity

Built for Fair, Transparent Campus Elections

Engineered to eliminate voting fraud, guarantee voter secrecy, and publish certified election results.

Admin Approval

Every student profile is verified by college administration before any ballot casting privileges are unlocked.

Secret Ballot

Voting records are decoupled from student identity. Nobody—not even the system administrator—can inspect individual candidate choices.

One Vote per Student

Strict database constraints and atomic ACID transactions reject repeat votes and race conditions at the engine level.

Digital Receipts

Voters receive a cryptographic verification token to confirm their ballot was counted, without exposing their choices.

How College Voting Works

A streamlined, 4-step workflow designed for student convenience.

1
Register Account

Fill in your college email, student roll number, department, and create a secure password.

2
Admin Verification

The election commission verifies your credentials against the active college roster.

3
Cast Secret Ballot

Review candidates, read their manifestos, and submit your confirmed vote during the active window.

4
Audited Results

Check live results once published by election officers, complete with vote percentages and winner declarations.
